AI Box Review Tool

Check boxes made by an AI model. Mark each box as Correct, Wrong label, Bad box, Duplicate or False alarm, then draw the objects the AI missed. In practice mode, the tool plants known errors in the boxes, so it can tell you how many of them you caught.

or

Practice set: load a COCO file with the correct boxes (for example, the COCO export from my first tool). The tool copies it, plants errors, and hides the answers until you press Check my review. Use a new seed each time you try, so you do not just remember the last one.

Click an AI box, then choose a verdict. For Wrong label, choose the right label. For Bad box, drag the box or its handles to fix it. Drag on empty space to add a box the AI missed.
